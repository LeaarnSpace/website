## About the role

Learning outcomes here are shaped by conditions almost nobody measures. As a Research Associate you will help design, run and write up the longitudinal studies that make those conditions visible.

Everything we learn is published, including when it is not flattering to us. You will see your work used by ministries, funders and schools, not filed away.

## What you will do

- Run interviews, surveys and short check-ins with parents, teachers and students.
- Keep cohorts engaged over time, so we can return to the same people year after year.
- Clean, analyse and document data, with consent and anonymisation built into the method.
- Draft briefs and findings that people designing programmes can read and use.
- Work with the product team so that what we learn shapes what we build.

## What we are looking for

- Two or more years in research, evaluation or data analysis, ideally in education or child development.
- Comfort with qualitative and quantitative methods, and honesty about what the data cannot say.
- Clear, plain writing.
- Care in handling data about children and families.

### Nice to have

- Experience with panel or longitudinal studies in Nigeria.
- Working knowledge of Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo or Pidgin.
